蓝色失重 / Weightless in Blue

Original wallpaper made for the Wallpaper Box official library.
Pool background and character animation atlas were generated specifically for
this project from the approved original visual concept. Character depicts an
adult athlete. Animation, camera, water, fish, plants and controls are rendered
locally using Canvas 2D. No network services, tracking, audio or runtime fonts
are required. The wallpaper contains exactly one animated swimmer.

Keyboard: Space pause, A follow, G garden, 1–7 chapters, arrows explore,
R restart, S hide interface, F fullscreen, H help, Escape close / reveal.
