蓝色失重 / Weightless in Blue

Original wallpaper made for the Wallpaper Box official library.
Pool background and complete character pose illustrations were generated
specifically for this project from the original visual concept. The character
depicts an adult athlete. Version 1.1.0 replaces the detached-part character
with seven complete illustrations and a continuous deformation mesh. Faces,
fingers and toes are rigid detail regions. Animation is intentionally gentle
to preserve the authored anatomy. A complete-artwork fallback is used when
WebGL is unavailable. Camera, water, fish, plants and controls use local
Canvas 2D rendering. No network services, tracking, audio or runtime fonts are
required. The wallpaper contains exactly one animated swimmer.

Keyboard: Space pause, A follow, G garden, 1–7 chapters, arrows explore,
R restart, S hide interface, F fullscreen, H help, Escape close / reveal.
