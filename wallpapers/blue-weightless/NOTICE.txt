蓝色失重 / Weightless in Blue

Original wallpaper made for the Wallpaper Box official library.
Pool background and complete character pose illustrations were generated
specifically for this project from the original visual concept. The character
depicts an adult athlete. Standing, diving, gliding and climbing use complete
illustrations with a detail-preserving mesh, or complete-artwork fallback.
Freestyle, breaststroke, backstroke and butterfly each use sixteen complete
authored drawings, registered to the same torso position. Each frame preserves
the drawn face, fingers and toes without separating or stretching limbs.
Breaststroke has a longer glide; butterfly uses two kicks per arm cycle.
Forward motion, water entry splashes and body movement follow stroke timing.
Stroke cycles are composited with Canvas 2D and also work without WebGL.
A missing motion atlas falls back to the corresponding complete illustration.
Camera, water, fish, plants and controls use local Canvas 2D rendering.
No network services, tracking, audio or runtime fonts are required. The
wallpaper contains exactly one animated swimmer.

Keyboard: Space pause, A follow, G garden, 1–7 chapters, arrows explore,
R restart, S hide interface, F fullscreen, H help, Escape close / reveal.
